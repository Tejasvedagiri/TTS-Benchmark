# The finish line: a marathon runner panting, gasping and cheering
Narrator: *The last hundred metres of a marathon. The crowd roars.*
Runner: *She pants hard, every breath ragged.* "Hah-- hah-- come on. Come on!"
Coach: *He shouts from the barrier.* "Go, Priya! Go! You've got this!"
Runner: *She crosses the line and collapses onto the grass.* "Hah... hah... I did it. Hhhh. I actually did it."
Coach: "Yes! Yes! Woo-hoo!" *He laughs and claps wildly.*
Runner: *Still gasping, she starts to laugh too.* "Ha ha! Never... hah... never again."
