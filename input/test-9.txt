# The spider: a scream, a gasp, then a relieved laugh
Narrator: *A quiet evening. A woman reaches for a book on the shelf.*
Hannah: *She screams.* "Aaaah! Spider! Spider! There's a huge spider!"
Tom: *He runs in from the kitchen.* "Where? Where is it?"
Hannah: *She gasps and points with a trembling finger.* "There! On the... on the..."
Tom: *He leans closer, then bursts out laughing.* "Hannah. It's a hair clip. Ha ha ha!"
Hannah: *A long sigh of relief.* "Phew. Oh my God." *She laughs, embarrassed.* "Don't tell anyone."
