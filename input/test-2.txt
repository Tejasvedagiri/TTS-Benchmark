# Winter soup: mom and son slurping and moaning with delight
Narrator: *A snowy winter evening. Two steaming bowls of tomato soup sit on the kitchen table.*
Mom: *She wraps her cold hands around the bowl and sips.* "Mmmmmm. Mmm. Oh, that's warm."
Leo: *A long, loud slurp.* "Mmmmmmm! Oh wow. Ten out of ten."
Mom: "Mmmh! So rich. So creamy." *She sighs, dreamy.* "Like a hug in a bowl."
Leo: *He slurps again, even louder.* "Ahhh. Soup. Is. Life."
Mom: "Leo! Manners." *She laughs, then slurps hers just as loudly.* "Mmm-mm-MMM!"
