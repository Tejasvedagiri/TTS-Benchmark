# The elevator: a businessman mumbling his presentation to himself
Narrator: *A busy office lobby. A man in a grey suit stares at the elevator doors, clutching a folder.*
Businessman: *He mutters under his breath.* "Okay. Good morning, everyone. Thank you for... no. Morning, team."
Businessman: "Q3 revenue is up twelve percent, driven by... um... driven by our, uh, our new..."
Narrator: *He flips through the folder, panicking slightly.*
Businessman: "Hmm. Hmm. Driven by the new enterprise accounts. Yes. Enterprise." *He exhales slowly.*
Narrator: *The elevator dings.*
Businessman: *He clears his throat and straightens his tie.* "Ahem. Showtime."
