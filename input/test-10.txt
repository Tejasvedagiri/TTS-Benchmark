# The bedtime story: a sleepy dad yawning and drifting off mid-sentence
Narrator: *A dim bedroom. A dad sits beside his son's bed with a picture book.*
Dad: "And the little bear said, I'm not tired at all." *A huge yawn.* "Haaahhh. Sorry."
Leo: "Keep reading, Daddy!"
Dad: *His voice gets slower and softer.* "So the bear... climbed the hill... and he... mmm... he..."
Narrator: *His head droops onto his chest. A gentle snore fills the room.*
Leo: *He whispers, then giggles.* "Daddy? Daddy, wake up. Hee hee. You fell asleep first."
Dad: *He jolts awake.* "Hm? Wha-- I'm awake! I'm awake. Where was I?"
