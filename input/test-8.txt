# The sick call: a blocked nose, sneezing and sniffling down the phone
Narrator: *Seven in the morning. A man wrapped in a blanket dials his boss.*
Sam: *His voice is thick and nasal.* "Hi, Linda. It's Sam. I don't think I can come in today."
Sam: "Ah... ah... ACHOO!" *He sniffles.* "Sorry. Sorry about that."
Boss: "Oh, Sam, you sound terrible. Stay home."
Sam: *He sniffs loudly and coughs.* "Cough, cough. Thanks. I'll check my email if I can... ah... ACHOO!"
Boss: *She chuckles.* "Do not check your email. Go back to bed."
