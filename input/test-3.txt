# The mechanic: hits his head on the bonnet, swears, throws the spanner
Narrator: *A cramped garage. A mechanic leans deep under the open bonnet of an old sedan.*
Mechanic: "Almost... got it... there we go."
Narrator: *He straightens up too fast and cracks his head on the bonnet.*
Mechanic: "Ouch! Ouch, ouch, ouch! Ahhh!" *He clutches the back of his head.*
Mechanic: "Damn this car! Damn this stupid, rusty car!"
Narrator: *He hurls the spanner across the garage. It clangs off the wall.*
Mechanic: *He groans and rubs his head.* "Hnnngh. Fine. Fine. I'll get the spanner."
