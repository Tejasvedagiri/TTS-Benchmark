# The sleepover: kids whispering and giggling after lights out
Narrator: *Midnight. Two girls lie in sleeping bags on a bedroom floor, torches under their chins.*
Maya: *She whispers.* "Psst. Zoe. Are you awake?"
Zoe: *She whispers back.* "Yes. Shh! Your mum will hear us."
Maya: "I put a frog in my brother's shoe." *She snorts.* "Hee hee hee!"
Zoe: *She clamps a hand over her mouth, shaking with laughter.* "Hnnph! Hee hee! Stop it!"
Narrator: *A door creaks open down the hall. Both girls freeze.*
Maya: *The quietest whisper.* "Pretend to sleep. Pretend to sleep."
