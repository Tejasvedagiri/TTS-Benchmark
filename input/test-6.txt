# Grandpa's joke: laughing at his own joke until he coughs
Narrator: *Sunday lunch. Grandpa sets down his fork and grins at the table.*
Grandpa: "Why did the scarecrow win an award? Because he was outstanding in his field!"
Grandpa: "Ha! Ha ha ha! Outstanding! Ha ha!" *He wheezes, slapping the table.*
Grandpa: *His laugh turns into a coughing fit.* "Ahem! Hack, hack! Oh dear. Oh dear me."
Ella: *She rolls her eyes but can't help a small laugh.* "Grandpa, you tell that one every week."
Grandpa: *He wipes a tear from his eye.* "And it gets funnier every week, sweetheart. Heh heh."
